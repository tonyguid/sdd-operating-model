Product Owner
Suggested ratio: 1 per 1–2 product domains
Own product vision, business requirements, and feature acceptance — bridge business stakeholders and engineering teams.
%Responsibility
25%Define and maintain product vision, business requirements, and user stories with clear acceptance criteria
20%Prioritize the product backlog based on business value, market needs, and stakeholder input
20%Collaborate with Architects to translate business requirements into spec-ready inputs
15%Validate delivered features against business intent and user needs (feature acceptance)
10%Communicate product direction and progress to business stakeholders
10%Coordinate with TPMs on release planning, roadmap alignment, and go-to-market timing

KPIs

Feature adoption & usageStakeholder satisfactionBacklog health (groomed vs. stale)Requirements clarity (spec rework rate)

Key Insight

In AI-augmented development, the PO's requirement clarity has outsized impact. Vague requirements cascade into vague specs, which produce poor AI output. POs must write precise, testable acceptance criteria — the quality of their input directly determines AI leverage.
