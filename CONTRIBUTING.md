# Contributing & Providing Feedback

This operating model is a living document. We're iterating in the open and want input from all stakeholders — architects, developers, TPMs, dev managers, and leadership.

## How to Give Feedback

### 1. Open an Issue (Recommended)

Best for general feedback, questions, or concerns.

1. Go to the [Issues tab](../../issues)
2. Click **New Issue** and select the **Feedback** template
3. Tag your issue with the appropriate label:
   - `feedback` — General feedback
   - `roles` — Feedback on role definitions or allocations
   - `process` — Feedback on the spec lifecycle or workflow
   - `metrics` — Feedback on KPIs or reporting
   - `question` — Questions or clarifications

### 2. Submit a Pull Request

Best for specific wording changes or additions.

1. Fork or branch from `main`
2. Make your changes
3. Open a PR with a clear description of what you changed and why
4. Tag the Chief Architect for review

### 3. Start a Discussion

Best for open-ended topics or brainstorming.

1. Go to the [Discussions tab](../../discussions)
2. Start a new thread in the appropriate category

## What We're Looking For

We especially want feedback on:

- **Role definitions:** Are the responsibilities and allocations realistic? What's missing?
- **Spec lifecycle:** Does the 7-phase flow make sense? Are there gaps or bottlenecks?
- **Executive metrics:** Are these the right things to measure? Are the targets reasonable?
- **Feasibility:** What are the biggest risks or blockers to adopting this model?
- **Team structure:** Are the suggested ratios (e.g., 1 architect per 2-3 domains) appropriate for our scale?

## Feedback SLA

- All issues will be triaged within **3 business days**
- Discussion threads will get an initial response within **5 business days**
- PRs will be reviewed within **1 week**

## Code of Conduct

Be constructive, specific, and solution-oriented. We're all working toward the same goal — building a better engineering organization.
