Technical Program Manager (TPM)
Suggested ratio: 1 per 3–4 product domains
Prioritize, sequence, and track spec-driven delivery — own the roadmap, reporting, and cross-team coordination.
%Responsibility
25%Prioritize spec backlog with product and architecture input
20%Track spec lifecycle (drafted → reviewed → implementing → verified → done)
20%Produce executive dashboards and status reports
15%Manage cross-team dependencies and sequencing
10%Facilitate sprint/cycle planning and retrospectives
10%Risk identification, escalation, and mitigation tracking

Key Artifacts

Roadmap with Spec TrackingExecutive DashboardsDependency MapsSprint/Cycle PlansRisk Registers

KPIs

Roadmap predictabilityCycle time (spec → done)Risk resolution rateReporting cadence & quality

Key Insight

TPMs report upward in business terms (features, milestones) while managing downward in spec terms. AI-driven velocity means estimation models must be recalibrated — things move faster but in different patterns.
